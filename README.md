#### Diagnosing and Rulebook
With the listed down symptoms, test results, and interviews, players have to navigate through the rulebook for the perfect diagnosis.

#### Decision Making
The gameplay heavily relies on decision making. Players have to make sure to give the right medication. The wrong medication or diagnosis ends with consequences or a penalty. Decision making is also needed in order to get time bonuses during patient banter.

#### Time Management
Players, on default, have only 1-minute a day to fulfill maximum possible patients. The game rewards them for their quick work but only if they diagnose correctly as the wrong move could earn a deduction in their overall time.

#### Known Issues

- Resolution works but I couldn't figure out how to make it borderless windowed. I advise using the smaller resolutions when using windowed.
- Music can possibly have different volumes when first opened. This is because the default volume assigned in the main menu is saved before you can set it.
- The blinking animation isn't that smooth because I didn't have time to polish each sprite.
- **IF TIME IS BROKEN** (Usually when deleting your saves after buying items), just press delete saves again.
